Our Story

Greek souvlaki, made right here in North Canterbury

Matt isn't Greek himself, but he grew up with family who taught him how to cook it properly. Yeah Boi Souvlaki is him bringing that to Rangiora: marinated meats grilled to order, warm pita and salads packed with fresh local produce.

Whether it's a quick lunch, the family's Friday night takeaway, or catering for your next event, we've got you sorted across Rangiora, Kaiapoi, Woodend, Oxford and greater Christchurch.

  • Fresh local produce, prepped daily
  • Greek recipes, learned the proper way
  • Locally owned & operated in Rangiora
