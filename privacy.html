Privacy Policy

How Yeah Boi Souvlaki handles the information you share with us.

This is placeholder content for demonstration purposes. Final policy should be reviewed by the business owner before going live.

1. Overview

Yeah Boi Souvlaki ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you contact us or place an order, and how we use it. We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 2020 (New Zealand).

2. What we collect

When you order or enquire, we may collect:

  • Your name and contact details (phone number or email address)
  • Order or catering details you provide
  • Delivery address, where relevant

We do not collect payment card details through this website.

3. How we use it

We use your information only to prepare and fulfil your order, arrange catering, respond to your enquiry, and keep in touch about your order. We do not sell your information.

4. Third parties

If you order through a delivery partner (such as Uber Eats or Delivereasy), your information is handled under that provider's own privacy policy. Our website embeds a Google Map, which is subject to Google's privacy policy.

5. Storage & security

We keep personal information only as long as needed to serve you and meet our legal obligations, and we take reasonable steps to keep it secure.

6. Your rights

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you. Contact us using the details below and we'll help.

7. Contact

Questions about your privacy? Call 027 504 0531 or reach us via the contact options on our website.
